FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Pro Creator?

Pro Creator is a sales management platform that brings together tools to organize clients, create proposals, track approvals and formalize digital contracts.

How does the guarantee work?

You have 7 days to try it in practice. If it doesn't fit your routine, just request a refund within the guarantee period, no red tape.

Can I use my own brand on proposals?

Yes. You can customize your sales proposals with your company's brand.

Do contracts have a digital signature?

Yes. Pro Creator offers digital contract signing through its Autentique integration.

Can I use my own contracts?

Yes. You can import PDF contracts and send them for digital signature.

Does Lifetime have a monthly fee?

No. Lifetime is a one-time payment of R$ 67.90, with no monthly fee and no auto-renewal. It includes up to 50 registered clients, 20 proposals, 10 contracts and 10 work orders per month, with unlimited receipts. If you need more capacity, monthly plans raise these limits.

Does my client need to install anything or create an account?

No. They don't need to install anything or create an account: the client opens the proposal straight from the link on a phone or computer, quickly and easily.

Does the digital signature cost extra?

No! Pro Creator offers digital signature via Autentique with no extra charge per signature, within your plan's contract limits.

Are my data and documents protected?

Pro Creator runs on Supabase Pro infrastructure, with authentication and access control. It also has a Privacy Policy and Terms of Use covering Brazil's data protection law (LGPD).

Does Pro Creator make backups?

Yes. The database has automatic daily backups, with restore options through the Supabase Pro infrastructure. Files in Storage are backed up separately.

Can I export my data if I stop using the platform?

Yes! Pro Creator lets you export client, proposal and contract information. The sales content you add remains yours, under the Terms of Use. PDFs of proposals, contracts and work orders can also be downloaded at any time.

Can I store old documents?

On Business you can import old contracts. Importing old proposals is in development; please confirm availability before subscribing.

Can several people sign the same contract?

Yes! Pro Creator supports multiple signers on one contract, for documents that need signatures from clients, managers, partners or witnesses.

Do I have to stop using WhatsApp?

No. Keep talking to your clients on WhatsApp as usual. Pro Creator organizes the sales steps after the quote request: sending proposals by link, tracking views and following through to the signed contract.

Can I add my price list?

Yes. Add products, services and prices to make new quotes faster and easier.

How does contract signing work?

The contract is signed digitally, right from the link sent to your client, and is legally valid under Brazilian law. Once signed, it is saved automatically in the client's history in your account.

Does Pro Creator integrate with other software?

Yes. Pro Creator integrates with Autentique for digital signature and, on Business and Enterprise plans, with Asaas for billing. We don't offer a public API or webhooks yet; custom integrations can be evaluated on request.
